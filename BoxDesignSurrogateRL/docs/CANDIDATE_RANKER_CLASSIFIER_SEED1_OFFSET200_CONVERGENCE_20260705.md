## Interpretation

This third seed-1 window again supports the same mechanism: learned candidate
ranking changes which exact MILP queries are spent early, and exact audit keeps
the final result on the same validation footing as exact staged greedy.

The uncached-box reduction is smaller than in seed1:test[0,100), but all
primary cost metrics remain lower at identical final PF and coverage. Across
seed1:test[0,100), seed1:test[100,200), and seed1:test[200,300),
ranker+audit preserves or improves final PF and full coverage, with aggregate
reductions of 14.1% in validations, 6.7% in uncached boxes, 6.5% in
Java/Gurobi subprocess time, and 7.4% in wall-clock time.
