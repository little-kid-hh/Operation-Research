## Interpretation

This second seed-1 window is positive but less dramatic than seed1:test[0,100).
It still supports the same mechanism: learned candidate ranking changes the
search path but exact MILP verification preserves final-quality validity while
reducing measured oracle work.

Across seed1:test[0,100) and seed1:test[100,200), ranker+audit preserves or
improves final PF and full coverage, with aggregate reductions of 12.7% in
validations, 7.7% in uncached boxes, 7.3% in Java/Gurobi subprocess time, and
7.7% in wall-clock time.
